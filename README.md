## About This Book

This book is different from every other Git book you will find.

It was written by a medical professional — not a software developer —
who learned Git from scratch in a single intensive session. That means
every explanation, every analogy, every "why does this matter" moment
comes from the perspective of a complete beginner who has been through
exactly what you are about to experience.

Whether you are a doctor, teacher, researcher, business professional
or aspiring developer — this book will take you from zero to confident
Git user, step by step, hands-on, with real commands on your own machine.

---

## Who This Book Is For

- Complete beginners who have never heard of Git
- Professionals who want to manage their work files better
- Researchers who want to version control their documents
- Anyone who wants to build a professional online presence
- Developers who want to fill the gaps in their Git knowledge

---

## How To Use This Book

Each chapter follows the same structure:

1. **Learning objectives** — what you will be able to do by the end
2. **Concepts explained** — with plain English analogies
3. **Hands-on exercises** — real commands on your own machine
4. **Common mistakes** — and exactly how to fix them
5. **Chapter assessment** — test yourself
6. **Answers** — at the end of each chapter

---

## Study Plans

### Beginner — 2 weeks
Read chapters 1-4 in week 1. Chapters 5-7 in week 2.

### Intermediate — 1 week
Skim chapters 1-2. Focus on chapters 3-8.

### Expert — Weekend
Jump to chapters 8-11 for advanced topics.
